About Us

Carrigon LLC is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business delivering management consulting, digital transformation, and data analytics to federal agencies. Founded by a West Point graduate and Army veteran with 10 years of military leadership and operational planning experience, Carrigon brings proven expertise in organizational change management, agile adoption, and mission-aligned digital solutions.

Core capabilities span IT and management consulting, digital transformation strategy, data science and quantitative risk modeling, cloud engineering and migration, DevSecOps and CI/CD pipeline design, application modernization, IT staffing, workforce training, and program management support. Our team pairs federal consulting experience — supporting DoD and civilian agency modernization — with quantitative analytics expertise from a top GSE's risk modeling organization.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at carrigon.com (the "Site"). Please read this policy carefully. Automatically collected information: When you visit the Site, we may automatically collect certain technical data including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit. This data is collected via standard web server logs and, if applicable, analytics tools.

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide requested information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Disclosure of Your Information

We may share your information in limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating the Site (e.g., email delivery, hosting) under confidentiality obligations.
