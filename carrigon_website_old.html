About Us

Carrigon LLC is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business delivering management consulting, digital transformation, and data analytics to federal agencies. Founded by a West Point graduate and Army veteran with 10 years of military leadership and operational planning experience, Carrigon brings proven expertise in organizational change management, agile adoption, and mission-aligned digital solutions.

Core capabilities span IT and management consulting, digital transformation strategy, data science and quantitative risk modeling, cloud engineering and migration, DevSecOps and CI/CD pipeline design, application modernization, workforce training, and program management support. Our team pairs federal consulting experience at Booz Allen Hamilton — supporting DoD and civilian agency modernization — with quantitative analytics expertise from Freddie Mac's risk modeling organization.
