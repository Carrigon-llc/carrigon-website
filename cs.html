About CarriSys

Veteran-led prime.
Enterprise-grade delivery.

CarriSys LLC brings the VA mission alignment of a combat veteran managing partner together with the enterprise IT delivery infrastructure of a 27-year firm — giving VA program offices a credentialed SDVOSB prime with real execution capacity behind it.

We compete under the Veterans First Contracting Program (38 U.S.C. 8127) and bring SST's full certification stack, staffing depth, and contract vehicles to every engagement.

The JV combines Carrigon's veteran credentials and program leadership with System Soft Technologies' enterprise delivery capability across IT staffing, software engineering, cybersecurity, operations, and AI governance.
